### Learning

Aster was started as a hands-on learning project with zero prior knowledge of modern web development.

AI was used as a development and learning partner throughout the process to help understand concepts, explore implementation approaches, debug problems, and iterate on the application.

The goal was not simply to generate a website, but to learn by building, understand the resulting software, and develop the ability to continue extending it independently.

### Direction

Aster is being developed as the central foundation for future software projects, experiments, milestones, and technical documentation.
